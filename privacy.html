BT&T Hub: privacy

The BT&T Hub Teams app is an internal tool of JMW & AG Ford & Sons Pty Ltd (Ballarat Trailers & Towbars) for its own staff. It is not offered to anyone else.

It signs you in with your BT&T Microsoft 365 account and reads BT&T's own SharePoint lists (such as Hub Jobs) as you. It shows only what your account can already see. It does not sell, share or send your information outside BT&T's Microsoft 365 tenant, does not store your sign-in, and keeps no data in this web page.
